# Student order flow (sign-in, details, items, pay, check, done, my orders)

Mode: Operate (the order steps), with the sign-in and closed pages as Persuade openings. Code-led (no image generation).
Audience/job: MDI students on phones ordering 1–2 garments with their name on the back in two minutes; form admins on laptops.
Constraints: one-screen Items step at 360×780; both round themes (Batch T-shirt, Winter Hoodies); MART name and garment tag kept; no invented scarcity, counts or claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A merch drop, not a form. The garment is the product shot, huge on black, and the student's own name is printed on it live. Refuses the category default: a white form with a small preview and a submit button.

OWN-WORLD: True black ground, white type, one loud colour per round that owns whole regions: Volt (#C8FF2E) for Batch T-shirt, Periwinkle (#9AA6FF, the real hoodie print colour Oliver shared 2026-11-27) for Winter Hoodies. Display in Big Shoulders Display (condensed, all-caps drop labels and huge headlines); UI text in Archivo; the back name in Michroma, the print face. Rotated stickers (price, size, the round's closing note) slapped on the product shot; a loud marquee band; the cream garment tag stays as the order card.

STORY: The student sees their garment big, sees their name land on it, picks colour/size/batch on the tag, pays, and leaves with a tag-shaped receipt.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone Items: top bar (MART, Order / My orders); a loud marquee band running the round title and its closing note; the product shot full width, about a third of the screen, garment centred with the price and size stickers on it and the colour swatches along its bottom edge; under it, the garment tag with size, back name, batch; the bottom bar with total, + Add, Pay (primary, loud colour). Laptop: product shot fills the left 60% at full height with stickers; tag and the order stack on the right. Sign-in: full-bleed black, huge condensed headline, the garment fan, loud marquee, Sign in with Google in the loud colour.

FORM: pinned whole by the user. seed: none (pinned) — "Streetwear drop: like a sneaker or merch drop site: huge product imagery, bold stickers and badges, 'limited run', marquee text, high-contrast black and one loud colour." ('limited run' translated to the round's real closing note: no stock limit exists.) Signature interaction: typing the back name prints it onto the garment and onto a sticker at once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
